PRACTICES

Daily rituals for the mind

Mindfulness 🌅

Morning Meditation

10 min

Start with intention. Guided breath awareness to ground your nervous system and set a clear focus for the day.

Breathwork 🫁

Box Breathing

5 min

Navy SEAL technique: 4 counts in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold. Activates the parasympathetic nervous system instantly.

Recovery 🌙

Sleep Ritual

20 min

Body scan, progressive muscle relaxation, and gratitude reflection to ease into restorative deep sleep.

Reflection ✍️

Journaling Prompt

15 min

Structured prompts for emotional processing, goal clarity, and self-compassion. Pen over screen, always.

Grounding 🌿

Nature Walk

30 min

A mindful forest bathing walk — leave headphones behind. Engage all senses. Cortisol drops measurably.

Boundaries 🚫

Digital Detox Hour

60 min

One screen-free hour per day. Read, cook, stretch, or simply be. Your dopamine receptors will thank you.
