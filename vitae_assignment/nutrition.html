DAILY PRINCIPLES

Four habits that change everything

💧

Hydrate First

Drink 500ml of water within 30 minutes of waking. Overnight dehydration affects metabolism and focus.

🌈

Eat the Rainbow

Aim for 5+ colors of fruits and vegetables daily. Each pigment delivers unique antioxidants.

🕒

Meal Timing

Align your largest meal with your peak activity window — mid-morning to mid-afternoon for most.

🧘

Mindful Eating

Sit, slow down, chew fully. It takes 20 minutes for satiety signals to reach your brain.
